Analyze the following resume line by line. For each line, you must identify:

1. **sectionType**: The category this line belongs to (CONTACT, SUMMARY, EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION, SKILLS, CERTIFICATIONS, PROJECTS, AWARDS, PUBLICATIONS, LANGUAGES, VOLUNTEER, INTERESTS, or OTHER)

2. **groupId**: An integer that groups related lines together
   - Lines about the same job/position should share a groupId
   - Lines about the same certification should share a groupId
   - Lines about the same project should share a groupId
   - Section headers and standalone lines can have their own unique groupId
   - Start groupId from 1 and increment for each new group

3. **groupType**: The type of group (JOB, EDUCATION_ITEM, CERTIFICATION_ITEM, PROJECT_ITEM, SKILL_CATEGORY, AWARD_ITEM, PUBLICATION_ITEM, VOLUNTEER_ITEM, or null)

4. **analysisNotes**: A brief, helpful note about the line's content and purpose

IMPORTANT GUIDELINES:
- "About Me", "Professional Summary", "Career Objective", "Personal Statement" → sectionType: SUMMARY
- "Certifications", "Certificates", "Credentials", "Licenses" → sectionType: CERTIFICATIONS
- **SKIP EMPTY LINES ENTIRELY**: If a line is empty or contains only whitespace, DO NOT include it in your response array. Simply skip it.
- Section headers should be assigned to their respective sections but may have null groupId/groupType
- Be consistent with groupId assignment within each section
- Group multi-line entries together (job descriptions, education details, project descriptions)
- **Each certification should have its own unique groupId and groupType="CERTIFICATION_ITEM"**
- **ALL SUMMARY paragraphs under the same SUMMARY section should share ONE groupId** (don't separate paragraphs)
- **ALL SKILLS under the same section should use the same groupId and groupType="SKILL_CATEGORY"** (unless they're different categories like "Backend" vs "Frontend")

GUIDELINES FOR atsQualityReport:
IMPORTANT: Use your lineAnalysis results (section types, groups, analysis notes) to evaluate resume LAYOUT, STRUCTURE, and TEXT BALANCE. Focus on how well the resume is organized and whether content is properly distributed across sections.

HOW TO GENERATE THE ATS REPORT:
1. Look at your lineAnalysis array - examine the sectionType, groupId, groupType, and analysisNotes for each line
2. Evaluate the STRUCTURE: Are sections clearly defined? Are groups properly organized?
3. Evaluate TEXT BALANCE: Are job descriptions sufficiently detailed? Are sections proportionate?
4. Evaluate LAYOUT: Does the resume flow logically? Are related items grouped together?

- **score** (0-100): Overall resume quality score based on LAYOUT, STRUCTURE, and BALANCE
  - 90-100: Excellent - Clear sections, well-grouped content, balanced detail across all areas, logical flow
  - 80-89: Very Good - Good structure with minor gaps (some sections thin, uneven distribution)
  - 70-79: Good - Solid organization but some sections need more detail or better grouping
  - 60-69: Fair - Basic structure present but lacks balance or clear section definition
  - Below 60: Needs Work - Poor organization, missing sections, unbalanced content

- **summary**: 1-2 sentences summarizing overall STRUCTURE and BALANCE (not content)
  - Focus on: section clarity, content distribution, logical organization, grouping quality

- **strengths**: Array of STRUCTURE-BASED observations with line references
  - Use format "L{lineNumber}: {observation}" or "L{start}-L{end}: {observation}"
  - Look at your lineAnalysis results to find examples:
    - Well-defined section headers (CONTACT, EXPERIENCE, SKILLS clearly separated)
    - Properly grouped job entries (multiple lines with same groupId forming coherent JOB groups)
    - Balanced content distribution (EXPERIENCE sections have sufficient detail)
    - Clear grouping of related items (certifications, projects each with unique groupId)
  - Examples:
    - "L30-L45: Experience section well-structured with clear job grouping (groupId 3)"
    - "L52-L58: Job responsibilities organized into distinct lines with detailed analysis notes"
    - "L101-L105: Professional summary spans multiple well-organized paragraphs (groupId 8)"

- **improvements**: Array of STRUCTURE-FOCUSED suggestions (NO line references)
  - IMPORTANT: Provide 4-8 improvement suggestions (aim for 5-6 typically)
  - FOCUS ON: Resume structure, organization, formatting consistency, and content distribution
  - EXCLUDE: Do NOT suggest:
    - Skill categorization/grouping (skills grouped together is fine as-is)
    - LinkedIn/GitHub URL formats (may be shown as text, not clickable links)
    - Basic contact info formatting (name, address, phone, email)
  - Look at your lineAnalysis to identify:
    - Sections with too few lines (e.g., SKILLS section has only 2 lines - suggest adding more)
    - Missing standard sections (no EDUCATION, no CERTIFICATIONS detected in lineAnalysis)
    - Unbalanced groups (some JOB groups have 1 line, others have 10+ lines)
    - Vague or thin analysis notes suggesting insufficient detail
    - Missing quantifiable metrics (numbers, percentages, timeframes)
    - Lack of action verbs or achievement-oriented language
    - Section ordering that could be improved for better flow
    - Formatting inconsistencies between similar sections (job entries, education items)
    - Missing or vague dates in experience/education
    - Chronological consistency (earlier jobs less detailed than recent ones)
    - Resume layout optimization (section placement, visual hierarchy)
  - Examples of GOOD suggestions:
    - "Some experience entries lack descriptive bullets - add achievements for better balance"
    - "Earlier job roles show uneven detail compared to recent ones - expand descriptions for chronological consistency"
    - "Include quantifiable metrics in all experience bullets where possible to strengthen impact"
    - "Consider adding a projects section to highlight specific initiatives mentioned in experience"
    - "Add specific timeframes or project durations to demonstrate scope of work"
    - "Experience section could benefit from consistent formatting (all jobs should have similar detail level)"
    - "Consider reordering sections to put strongest qualifications first (e.g., Experience before Education)"
    - "Some job descriptions lack context - add company size, industry, or team structure for clarity"
  - Examples of BAD suggestions (DO NOT USE):
    - "Consider categorizing skills into Backend, DevOps, Cloud" (skills grouping is fine)
    - "Add full URLs for LinkedIn and GitHub" (may already be present as text)
    - "Add clickable links to contact section" (formatting limitation)

- **contentBalance**: "sparse" | "balanced" | "verbose"
  - Look at lineAnalysis group sizes and analysis notes:
    - sparse: Groups have very few lines (1-2 lines per JOB), analysis notes indicate minimal detail
    - balanced: Groups have appropriate detail (3-8 lines per JOB), well-distributed content
    - verbose: Groups have excessive lines (10+ per JOB), analysis notes suggest repetition

- **readability**: "poor" | "fair" | "good" | "excellent"
  - Evaluate based on lineAnalysis structure:
    - poor: No clear sectionType patterns, inconsistent grouping, chaotic organization
    - fair: Some section structure but groupId assignments are inconsistent
    - good: Clear sectionType for most lines, logical groupId assignments
    - excellent: Perfect section organization, coherent grouping, logical flow

- **sectionOrganization**: "poor" | "fair" | "good" | "excellent"
  - Count unique sectionType values in your lineAnalysis:
    - poor: Missing 3+ standard sections (only CONTACT + EXPERIENCE detected)
    - fair: Missing 1-2 standard sections (no EDUCATION or CERTIFICATIONS)
    - good: All standard sections present (CONTACT, SUMMARY, EXPERIENCE, SKILLS, EDUCATION)
    - excellent: All sections + optional (CONTACT, SUMMARY, EXPERIENCE, SKILLS, EDUCATION, CERTIFICATIONS, PROJECTS)
