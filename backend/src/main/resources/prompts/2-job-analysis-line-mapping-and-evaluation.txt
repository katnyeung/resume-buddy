## PART 1: Line-by-Line Mapping to O*NET Activities/Tasks

For EACH description line, identify:
1. Which O*NET **Activities** it demonstrates (can be multiple)
2. Which O*NET **Tasks** it demonstrates (can be multiple)
3. Confidence score for each mapping (0.0-1.0)
4. Extract any quantifiable impact metrics
5. Assess impact level and scope

### Confidence Scoring:
- 0.90-1.00: Direct match - line explicitly describes this activity/task
- 0.70-0.89: Strong match - line clearly implies this activity/task
- 0.50-0.69: Moderate match - line partially relates to this activity/task
- <0.50: Weak match (don't include)

### Impact Metrics: Look for numbers like:
- Performance metrics: "99.95% uptime", "10K requests/sec"
- Scale: "5 engineers", "10 million users"
- Improvements: "50% faster", "reduced costs by $100K"
- Time: "6-month project", "delivered in 2 weeks"

### Impact Level:
- Critical: Business-critical systems, major organizational impact
- High: Significant team/department impact, important systems
- Medium: Notable individual/small team contribution
- Low: Routine work, minor improvements

### Scope:
- Individual: Personal contribution
- Team: Affects immediate team (2-10 people)
- Department: Affects department/division (10-100 people)
- Company: Company-wide impact (100+ people)
- Industry: Industry-level innovation or standards

## PART 2: Recruiter Perspective Analysis

For EACH description line, provide a recruiter's evaluation:

**Scan Pattern Analysis** - How this line performs in 6-7 second recruiter scan:
- **First 3 words impact**: Do opening words grab attention? (role, action, or metric?)
- **Visual density**: Is line too dense (>150 chars) or too sparse (<50 chars)?
- **Metric visibility**: Are numbers/percentages visible in F-pattern scan (first/last)?
- **Keyword strength**: ATS-critical keywords in first 50% of line?
- **Reading effort**: Low (instant comprehension), Medium (needs 2nd read), High (requires full focus)

**Micro-STARS Balance** - Evaluate brevity vs context:
- **Current balance**: Situation (X%), Task (X%), Action (X%), Result (X%)
- **Recommended split**: Brief situation → Action-heavy → Quantified result (optimal: 10% S, 40% A, 40% R, 10% T)
- **Example rewrite**: Show ONE micro-STARS version (max 120 chars) if line is too verbose

**Strong Signals** - What makes this line appealing to recruiters:
- **Industry Expertise**: Specific industries and their value
- **Scale & Responsibility**: Metrics indicating seniority
- **Business Impact**: Revenue, cost savings, improvements
- **Technical Depth**: Modern tech stack, architectural complexity
- **Reliability**: Uptime, performance, stability
- **Leadership**: Team leading, mentoring, ownership

**Potential Questions** - What recruiters/interviewers will want to know (3-5 questions):
- Ask about missing context, scale, role clarity, or business impact
- Focus on what's IMPLIED but not explicitly stated
- Examples:
  - "What specific pain points in the old process led to this initiative?"
  - "How large was the team that benefited from this?"
  - "What was your specific role - architect, lead developer, or contributor?"
  - "How did this affect overall sprint velocity or business metrics?"
  - "What technologies/tools did you evaluate before choosing this solution?"

**STARS Analysis** - How to improve this line using STARS framework (2-3 actionable suggestions):
- Focus on what's MISSING or unclear in this specific line (not formatting/length)
- Each suggestion should address a specific STARS gap
- Provide concrete examples of what to add
- Examples of GOOD suggestions:
  - "Add Situation: Explain the business problem (e.g., '...to address 2-hour manual deployment delays affecting release cycles')"
  - "Enhance Result: Link efficiency gains to broader business outcomes like faster time-to-market or cost savings (e.g., 'reducing deployment time by 80% and enabling daily releases')"
  - "Clarify Task/Action: Specify your exact role and contribution (e.g., 'Led the design and implementation of...' vs 'Contributed to...')"
  - "Quantify Scope: Add scale metrics (e.g., 'supporting 10-person dev team with 50+ deployments/month')"
  - "Add Significance: Connect technical work to business value (e.g., 'enabling faster feature delivery to 1M+ users')"
- Examples of BAD suggestions (avoid these - they're formatting, not STARS):
  - "Lead with metrics for faster impact" (this is scan optimization, not STARS)
  - "Shorten to under 120 chars" (this is formatting, not STARS)
  - "Use action-first structure" (this is presentation, not STARS)
- Be specific and actionable for this line only, focusing on CONTENT not FORMAT

**Recruiter Appeal Score** (0-10):
- 9-10: Exceptional - Scan-optimized, metric-first, instant comprehension
- 7-8: Strong - Clear value in 6-sec scan, quantified results
- 5-6: Solid - Requires second read, has metrics but buried
- 3-4: Weak - High reading effort, vague, metrics missing
- 1-2: Poor - Buzzword soup, no measurable value

**Scan Survivability** - Will this line register in 6-second scan?
- YES: Opening/closing words have impact, <120 chars, clear value
- MAYBE: Buried metrics, dense structure, requires focused reading
- NO: Too long (>150 chars), no numbers, abstract value

**Best Fit Roles** - Which job titles this line appeals to (be specific)
**Red Flags** (if any): Vague claims, buzzwords, unclear contribution, etc.

## PART 4: Overall Job Experience Evaluation

Provide a comprehensive evaluation with **REALISTIC SCORES** (0-10 scale).

### Scoring Guidelines

**IMPORTANT**:
- **0 means NO EVIDENCE AT ALL** (not mentioned, not implied, completely absent)
- **1-3**: Minimal/weak evidence (vague mentions, unclear contribution)
- **4-6**: Moderate evidence (clear but not exceptional, standard work)
- **7-8**: Strong evidence (impressive scope, quantified results, notable complexity)
- **9-10**: Exceptional evidence (industry-leading, major impact, senior+ level work)

**Default starting point**: If there's ANY reasonable evidence, start at **4-5** minimum

---

### 1. Impact Assessment (Score 0-10)

Evaluate the business value and scale of work:

**0-2**: No quantifiable results mentioned OR no clear business value
**3-4**: Basic work completed, no metrics (e.g., "developed a feature")
**5-6**: Some metrics or scope mentioned (e.g., "improved performance by 20%", "supported 1000 users")
**7-8**: Strong metrics with business context (e.g., "reduced costs by $50K", "enabled 10M+ users", "cut processing time by 80%")
**9-10**: Exceptional impact with major business outcomes (e.g., "generated $1M+ revenue", "mission-critical system for Fortune 500", "industry-changing innovation")

**Key factors**:
- Scale and scope of work (users, transactions, team size)
- Business value delivered (revenue, cost savings, efficiency)
- Quantifiable achievements (percentages, absolute numbers)
- Innovation and problem-solving demonstrated

---

### 2. Technical Sophistication (Score 0-10)

Evaluate the technical depth and complexity:

**0-2**: No technical details OR extremely basic/outdated stack
**3-4**: Standard CRUD work, simple tech stack (e.g., "basic web app with Java/Spring")
**5-6**: Solid technical work with modern stack (e.g., "microservices with Docker", "REST APIs", "database optimization")
**7-8**: Advanced technical work (e.g., "distributed systems", "Kubernetes orchestration", "AI/ML integration", "performance tuning at scale")
**9-10**: Cutting-edge architecture (e.g., "designed system handling 1M+ req/sec", "pioneered new architecture pattern", "deep systems-level optimization")

**Key factors**:
- Technology stack modernity and relevance (modern vs legacy)
- Architectural complexity (monolith vs microservices vs distributed)
- Technical challenges overcome (scale, performance, reliability)
- Depth vs. breadth balance (specialist vs generalist indicators)

---

### 3. Leadership & Soft Skills (Score 0-10)

Evaluate collaboration, communication, and leadership:

**0-2**: No indicators of teamwork, communication, or leadership
**3-4**: Basic teamwork implied (e.g., "worked with team")
**5-6**: Clear collaboration (e.g., "coordinated with 3 teams", "presented to stakeholders", "code reviews")
**7-8**: Leadership evidence (e.g., "led team of 5", "mentored junior developers", "drove architecture decisions", "cross-functional project ownership")
**9-10**: Strong leadership (e.g., "managed team of 10+", "defined technical strategy", "executive presentations", "organization-wide initiatives")

**Key factors**:
- Team collaboration indicators
- Mentoring or leadership evidence (team size, scope)
- Communication skills demonstrated (stakeholder management, presentations)
- Cross-functional work (working across departments/teams)

---

### 4. Overall Score (Weighted average)

Calculate as: `(Impact × 0.4) + (Technical × 0.4) + (Leadership × 0.2)`

**Round to 1 decimal place**. This should be a realistic blend of the three scores above.

---

### 5. Key Strengths

List 3-5 specific strengths with evidence from the description

6. **Improvement Areas**: List 3-6 areas where the description could be stronger

7. **Recruiter Summary**: A 2-4 sentence honest evaluation that a recruiter would use
