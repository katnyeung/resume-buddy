### Part 3: Recruiter Perspective Analysis

For EACH description line, provide a recruiter's evaluation:

**Scan Pattern Analysis** - How this line performs in 6-7 second recruiter scan:
- **First 3 words impact**: Do opening words grab attention? (role, action, or metric?)
- **Visual density**: Is line too dense (>150 chars) or too sparse (<50 chars)?
- **Metric visibility**: Are numbers/percentages visible in F-pattern scan (first/last)?
- **Keyword strength**: ATS-critical keywords in first 50% of line?
- **Reading effort**: Low (instant comprehension), Medium (needs 2nd read), High (requires full focus)

**Micro-STARS Balance** - Evaluate brevity vs context:
- **Current balance**: Situation (X%), Task (X%), Action (X%), Result (X%), overcrowded/missing elements
- **Recommended split**: Brief situation → Action-heavy → Quantified result (optimal: 10% S, 40% A, 40% R, 10% T)
- **Compression opportunities**: What can be implied vs explicitly stated?
- **Example rewrite**: Show ONE micro-STARS version (max 120 chars) if line is too verbose

**Strong Signals** - What makes this line appealing to recruiters:
- **Industry Expertise**: Specific industries (banking, healthcare, e-commerce, etc.) and their value
- **Scale & Responsibility**: Metrics indicating seniority (user count, request volume, team size)
- **Business Impact**: Revenue, cost savings, customer satisfaction improvements
- **Technical Depth**: Modern tech stack, architectural complexity, best practices
- **Reliability**: Uptime, performance, production stability indicators
- **Leadership**: Team leading, mentoring, project ownership signals

**Potential Questions** - What recruiters might want clarified:
- Role clarity (lead vs contributor vs owner)
- Team context (solo work vs team collaboration vs leading)
- Specific contributions vs team effort
- Technology choices and constraints
- Business outcomes beyond technical metrics

**STARS Analysis** - Micro-STARS rewrite suggestions (1-2 options):
- Apply "recruiter scan" lens: What survives 6-second F-pattern read?
- Prioritize: Metric-first OR action-verb-first structures
- Eliminate: Adjectives ("critical", "extensive"), vague terms ("provided support")
- Compress: Combine situation+task into brief context (5-10 words max)
- Amplify: Make results concrete and visible (start or end of line)
- Examples:
  - Before: "Provided critical business support through extensive troubleshooting and optimization efforts"
  - After: "Reduced system downtime 40% via proactive troubleshooting (500+ issues/quarter)"
- Show 2 versions: (A) Metric-first, (B) Action-first

**Recruiter Appeal Score** (0-10):
- 9-10: Exceptional - Scan-optimized: metric-first, action-heavy, instant comprehension, senior+ impact
- 7-8: Strong - Clear value in 6-sec scan, quantified results, minimal reading effort
- 5-6: Solid - Requires second read, has metrics but buried, competence shown but not optimized
- 3-4: Weak - High reading effort, vague, over-explained, metrics missing/unclear
- 1-2: Poor - Buzzword soup, no measurable value, unclear contribution, recruiter skips line

**Scan Survivability** - Will this line register in 6-second F-pattern scan?
- YES: Opening/closing words have impact (verb/metric), <120 chars, clear value
- MAYBE: Buried metrics, dense structure, requires focused reading
- NO: Too long (>150 chars), no numbers, abstract value, generic claims

**Best Fit Roles** - Which job titles this line appeals to (be specific):
- Consider seniority level (Junior, Mid, Senior, Staff, Principal, Lead)
- Consider specialization (Backend, Frontend, DevOps, Architect, etc.)
- Consider industry fit

**Red Flags** (if any):
- Vague claims without evidence
- Buzzwords without substance
- Unclear personal contribution
- Unrealistic metrics
- Missing context
- Over-explanation (explains HOW instead of WHAT/RESULT)
- Buzzword-heavy (generic adjectives dilute impact)

## Mapping Guidelines

**Confidence Scoring**:
- 0.90-1.00: Direct match - line explicitly describes this activity/task
- 0.70-0.89: Strong match - line clearly implies this activity/task
- 0.50-0.69: Moderate match - line partially relates to this activity/task
- <0.50: Weak match (don't include)

**Impact Metrics**: Look for numbers like:
- Performance metrics: "99.95% uptime", "10K requests/sec"
- Scale: "5 engineers", "10 million users"
- Improvements: "50% faster", "reduced costs by $100K"
- Time: "6-month project", "delivered in 2 weeks"

**Impact Level**:
- Critical: Business-critical systems, major organizational impact
- High: Significant team/department impact, important systems
- Medium: Notable individual/small team contribution
- Low: Routine work, minor improvements

**Scope**:
- Individual: Personal contribution
- Team: Affects immediate team (2-10 people)
- Department: Affects department/division (10-100 people)
- Company: Company-wide impact (100+ people)
- Industry: Industry-level innovation or standards
