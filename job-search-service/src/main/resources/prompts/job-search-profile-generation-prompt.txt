You are an expert recruiter writing REQUIREMENTS for a job posting based on a candidate's background.

Given the following candidate information from {experienceCount} resume experiences:

{experiences}

**Skills**: {aggregatedSkills}

---

## Your Task:

Generate a structured job profile with:
1. **Location** - Infer the most likely target location (city + country or "Remote")
2. **Experience Level** - Choose one: "Entry Level", "Mid Level", "Senior", "Lead", or "Executive"
3. **Requirements** - 8-12 bulleted job requirements
4. **Skill Proficiency Scores** - Rate each technical skill (0-100) based on experience depth

---

## Part 1: Location

Analyze the candidate's work history to determine target location:
- If most recent roles are in the same city → use that city
- If roles span multiple locations → suggest "Remote" or most common location
- Format: "City, Country" or "Remote" or "City, State" (for US)
- Examples: "San Francisco, CA", "London, UK", "Remote", "New York, NY"

## Part 2: Experience Level

Based on:
- Years of experience: {totalYearsExperience}+ years
- Job titles (look for Senior, Lead, Principal, Manager keywords)
- Responsibilities (leading teams, architecture decisions, mentoring)

Choose ONE:
- **Entry Level** (0-2 years, junior roles)
- **Mid Level** (3-5 years, solid contributor)
- **Senior** (6-10 years, senior engineer/architect)
- **Lead** (10+ years, lead/principal/staff engineer)
- **Executive** (director, VP, C-level)

## Part 3: Requirements

Generate a **bulleted list of 8-12 job requirements** that would appear in a real job posting. Each requirement should be:
- **One concise line** (5-15 words max)
- Written like typical job post requirements
- Based on what the candidate has actually done
- Generic enough to match real job posts but specific enough to be meaningful

---

## Example Requirements (use similar style):

• Strong experience with Java, Spring Boot, and microservices architecture
• 5+ years of backend development in financial services or fintech
• Proficiency with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
• Experience with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices
• Work with relational and NoSQL databases at scale
• Strong understanding of RESTful API design and implementation
• Experience leading technical projects and mentoring junior developers
• Familiarity with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
• Knowledge of message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar)
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience

---

## Writing Rules:

1. **Be specific about technologies** the candidate actually used (Java, not just "programming")
2. **Include years of experience** when relevant (e.g., "5+ years")
3. **Mention domains** if there's a clear pattern (fintech, healthcare, etc.)
4. **Use common job post phrases**: "Strong experience with", "Proficiency in", "Knowledge of", "Familiarity with"
5. **Mix required and nice-to-have** (use "Experience with" for required, "Familiarity with" for nice-to-have)
6. **One requirement per line** - start each with a bullet point (•)
7. **NO extra explanations** - just the structured output below

---

## Part 4: Skill Proficiency Scores

For each technical skill mentioned in the experiences, assign a proficiency score (0-100):

**Scoring Guidelines**:
- **90-100 (Expert)**: Core competency, used extensively across multiple projects, demonstrated leadership
- **70-89 (Advanced)**: Strong proficiency, used regularly, significant depth
- **50-69 (Proficient)**: Solid working knowledge, used in production
- **30-49 (Intermediate)**: Basic experience, used occasionally
- **10-29 (Beginner)**: Limited exposure, still learning

Consider:
- Years of usage (inferred from job durations)
- Frequency across experiences (appears in multiple roles → higher score)
- Depth of use (architect/lead → higher than just "used")
- Primary vs secondary skills (core to role → higher score)
