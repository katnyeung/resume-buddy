## 7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

### Access (Article 15)
- Get a copy of your data
- Free once per year

### Rectification (Article 16)
- Correct inaccurate data
- Complete incomplete data

### Erasure (Article 17)
- Delete your account and data
- Exceptions: legal obligations, pending transactions

### Restriction (Article 18)
- Limit processing while disputes resolved

### Portability (Article 20)
- Export your data in JSON format

### Object (Article 21)
- Opt-out of marketing
- Object to legitimate interests processing

### Automated Decision-Making (Article 22)
- Our AI provides suggestions only
- No legally significant automated decisions

**To exercise rights**: Email privacy@resumebuddy.cv  
**Response time**: Within 30 days  
**ID Verification**: May be required for security
